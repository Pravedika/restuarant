About Our Story

A Legacy of Fine Gastronomy & Elegant Ambiance

Our Roots

How Haveli Began

"A passion for culinary excellence that started in a small rustic kitchen and evolved into an award-winning premium venue."

Founded in 2012 by Chef Henri Laurent, Haveli began as a dream to bring fine-dining quality to a comfortable, stylish environment. Chef Henri, having worked in three-starred Parisian kitchens, wanted to break the stiffness of classic restaurants while retaining their meticulous standards of cooking and pairing.

Over the past decade, we have expanded our location, installed our own in-house coffee roasting machinery, built a state-of-the-art temperature-controlled wine room, and continuously refined our menus. Yet, our core philosophy remains unchanged: serve exceptional food, with passion and zero compromise.

Our Mission

To excite and inspire our guests by delivering meticulously crafted plates, premium single-origin coffees, and curated wine pairings, all while supporting sustainable local farming and delivering warm, elite service.

Our Vision

To be globally recognized as a pioneer in modern, approachable fine dining, creating a sanctuary where gastronomic art, cutting-edge interior design, and ecological mindfulness coexist beautifully.

Our Journey

Milestones & Achievements

  • 2012 - Grand Launch

    The Dream Materializes

    Chef Henri Laurent establishes L'Ambroisie as a small 30-seat boutique cafe and bistro in Gourmet Heights, immediately winning local praise for its exceptional French pastry selection.

  • 2017 - Expansion & Roastery

    Expanding the Craft

    We acquire the neighboring property, tripling our seating capacity, adding an open kitchen concept, and installing our in-house specialty coffee roasting plant to deliver single-origin coffee masterclasses.

  • 2023 - Michelin Guide Recognition

    Earning Global Recognition

    L'Ambroisie is officially added to the prestigious Michelin Guide Guide, recognized for its commitment to ingredient sustainability and innovative culinary techniques.
